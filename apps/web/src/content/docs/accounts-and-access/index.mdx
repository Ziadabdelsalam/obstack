# Accounts and access

An account is a person: a name, an email address to sign in with, and a
password. It owns one organization, and that organization owns one workspace —
the tenant every screen in the product reads. Signing up creates all three in
one transaction, so there is no account without its workspace and no workspace
without its owner.

## Signing up and signing in

Signup asks for a name, an email address and a password of 8 to 128
characters, and nothing else. The address you type is the address the account
has: obstack sends no email of any kind, so nothing is verified and nothing has
to be clicked. The organization is named after you at signup, and the product
has no control to rename it.

Sign in with the address and the password. Attempts are limited per address —
5 signups an hour and 10 sign-ins every 10 minutes — and a refused attempt says
so rather than pretending the password was wrong.

## Your account

**Account**, in the top-right menu, is you as distinct from the workspace:

- **Name** — shown in the top bar and on the members roster of every
  organization you belong to.
- **Picture** — a PNG, JPEG or WebP. With JavaScript on, the picker shrinks it
  to 128 pixels before it is uploaded; either way it is judged against 256 KB
  and against its bytes, not its file name. It is shown beside your name in the
  top bar and on the roster, to the people who share an organization with you,
  and removing it shows your initials again.
- **Email** — the address you sign in with. Changing it applies at once, with
  no confirmation, and it is the address invitations are matched against: an
  invitation sent to your old address stops working for this account once you
  change it. An address that already belongs to another account is refused.
- **Password** — changed with the current one in hand, within the same 8 to
  128 characters. The same step can sign out every other session.
- **Sessions** — every browser signed in to your account, with when it signed
  in, when it expires, and the address the request arrived from as obstack saw
  it (a proxy's, or none). Any session but the current one is ended from the
  list; the current one is ended from the top bar's *Sign out*. An ended
  session is over on its next request.
- **Organizations** — every organization your account belongs to, with the
  role your membership holds.

Password attempts and email changes are limited per account, whatever address
they come from: 5 password attempts every 10 minutes and 5 email changes an
hour.

## Organizations, workspaces and teammates

A session reads one workspace at a time. By default it is the workspace of the
organization the account **owns**; every other organization the account belongs
to is a workspace it can switch to. The **switcher** sits under the workspace
label in the sidebar, lists each organization with your role in it, and appears
only when there is more than one; a switch is remembered until the next one.
Accepting an invitation adds an organization to that list and changes nothing
until you switch to it from the sidebar.

Teammates arrive by invitation, from **Settings → Members**. An invitation is a
link, not an email — obstack sends none — so the owner copies it out of the
pending list and delivers it however they like. It works only for an account
whose email address matches the invited one, it expires 48 hours after it was
issued, and it can be cancelled from the same list until it is accepted.

Accepting adds the invitee to the inviter's organization as a `member`: they
appear on its roster, it appears on their account page and in their switcher,
and their session keeps reading their own workspace until they switch. A member
who has switched can do everything in that workspace its owner can — read every
surface, issue and revoke keys, change the plan — except invite: inviting and
cancelling invitations stay with the owner, and the Members tab says so rather
than offering a form that would be refused. There is no member removal and no
role change in the product.

## Keys are for programs

An account signs in to the product. A **key** is what a program sends with — an
exporter, a collector, an agent — and it belongs to the workspace, not to a
person. It is issued in **Settings → API keys** with a scope: `ingest` to send
telemetry, `read` or `setup` to reach the workspace through the
[MCP endpoint](/docs/mcp). The token is shown once, and a key is revoked from
the same list; a revoked key stops being accepted within 30 seconds. The
[quickstart](/docs/quickstart) issues your first `ingest` key and shows it
inside the snippet that uses it.

Everything an account cannot do — recover a forgotten password, verify an
address, delete itself — is written down with the rest of the absences on
[what obstack does not do](/docs/what-obstack-does-not-do).
