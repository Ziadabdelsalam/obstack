# Plans and billing

Two plans exist. These four columns are the whole catalog — every number below
is a column in the product's own plan table, and nothing in the product defines
a plan anywhere else.

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $49 / month |
| Events per month | 50,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Retention | 7 days | 30 days |
| Explain runs per month | 20 | 200 |

A workspace that has never touched billing is on free, with no row having to
exist for it.

**There is no Scale plan, no per-event overage and no seat count in the catalog
today.** If you have seen a third tier, a price per additional block of events,
or a seat limit quoted anywhere, it is not something this product charges or
enforces.

## What counts as an event

Spans and log records. The usage meter on the **Billing & usage** tab in
settings shows exactly that pair against your plan's quota, and the billing
period is the UTC calendar month.

## Over quota, ingestion degrades — it does not stop

Crossing the event quota is deliberately **not** a refusal. An over-quota
workspace keeps getting `200`s and keeps seeing a tenth of its traffic, whole
traces at a time: obstack head-samples, one trace in ten survives, and **a
sampled-out trace is dropped whole while every trace that survives stays
complete**. Nothing is truncated mid-trace.

A hard cut-off would make the product go dark exactly when someone is trying to
work out why it is busy. What the degradation costs is counted — in the
operator's metrics, and in the per-key health rows the product shows, where it
reads as sampling rather than as errors.

[Retention](/docs/retention) is enforced separately, by a daily sweep against
the same plan column.

## Payment

**Polar is the merchant of record.** Checkout, payment and invoices are handled
by Polar; obstack stores which plan you are on, never a card. There is no
invoice list in the product, because it would either be empty forever or be a
second copy of Polar's.

The upgrade form names a plan and nothing else — the workspace comes from your
session on the server and the price comes from Polar's own checkout, so neither
is forgeable from the page.
